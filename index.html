Every muscle trains twice a week — Upper A+B, Lower A+B
🔥 Warm-up — mandatory before every session
8-10 minutes
  • 5 minutes light cardio (brisk walk / stationary bike)
  • Joint rotations: shoulder, hip, knee — 10 reps each direction
  • 1-3 warm-up sets at light weight (40-60% of working weight) before your first compound movement
Rule: 8-10 minutes of warm-up. Choose movements that don't cause pain, and load up gradually through the prep sets.
Moves like Cat-Cow or Glute Bridge can help before Lower days — try them, drop them if they're not comfortable.
Double Progression — the progress rule:
Every exercise has a rep range (e.g. 3×8-12). The method:
1️⃣ Keep the same weight until you hit the top of the range (12) on every set
2️⃣ Only then increase the weight by the smallest increment available at the gym
3️⃣ Drop back to the bottom of the range (8) and repeat

Why not a percentage? A 5% jump on Lateral Raise could be a massive leap. The range protects against that.

⚠️ Deload: failure (RIR 0) in two different weeks on the same exercise → cut the weight 10% for a week
🔄 Every 6 weeks → a deload week (50% volume, same movements)

Rest times between sets:
🏋️ Compound exercises: 2-3 minutes · 💪 Isolation: 60-90 seconds · 🧘 Core: 45-60 seconds

🔄 RDL return — cautious comeback protocol (light weight)

Week 1-2: very light weight (40-50% of normal), 3×10, slow and controlled movement. Stop immediately if you feel pain during the movement.

After every session, check two things:
✔️ No pain during execution
✔️ No deterioration within 24-48 hours after (tomorrow matters more than today)

✅ Two pain-free sessions in a row → increase weight gradually (normal Double Progression)
⚠️ Pain during or after → switch back to Leg Curl immediately, and if it persists see a physiotherapist or doctor.
